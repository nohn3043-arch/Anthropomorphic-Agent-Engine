##  The soul does not exist.  
## The mind is not mysterious. 

**Anthropomorphic Agent Engine is a production-grade framework designed to build AI agents with human-like personality, memory, emotion, and behavioral consistency. It transforms static role settings into dynamic, evolving characters that remember, get tired, change over time, and follow strict rules — suitable for games, virtual humans, customer service, education, and enterprise AI applications.**

**Existing AI companions and agents often “change” over time:
their memory becomes unstable, their statements contradict each other,
and their emotions drift.
They may look human, but they fail the test of time.
This engine addresses exactly that problem —
the collapse, drift, and loss of coherence that occur
when agents are expected to exist and interact over the long term.**

## 🚀 Core Features
### 1. Dynamic Personality System
- Affinity - Driven Evolution: Character traits change smoothly according to the relationship intimacy, which ranges from 0.0 to 1.0. This simulates the entire process from being a stranger to becoming a trusted partner. In addition, dynamic psychological parameter intervals could also be incorporated. For example, certain internal psychological parameters vary within intervals such as [0, 100] along with the change of the relationship intimacy, which further enriches the dynamic change mechanism of the personality system.

- **Multi-Mode Psychological Curves**:
  - ✅ **Linear**: Steady, predictable changes
  - ✅ **Sigmoid (S-Curve)**: Realistic "slow → fast → slow" emotional shifts (e.g., cold → softening → devoted)
  - ✅ **Step Function**: Abrupt changes at thresholds, representing principles, bottom lines, or role masks
- **Trait Mutual Exclusion**: Contradictory emotions automatically suppress each other — only the dominant feeling controls behavior, eliminating logical conflicts.
- **Scene & Target Lock**: Different personalities activate based on *who* is speaking and *where* the conversation happens.

### 2. Life Simulation Layer
- **Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve**: Relationships naturally fade over time without interaction; trust and familiarity require continuous maintenance.
- **Cognitive Energy System**:
  - High-consumption interactions (conflicts, deep talks) drain energy → fatigue leads to simpler, more instinct-driven behavior.
  - Relaxed chats restore energy → recover composure and complexity.
- **Trauma & Permanent Mutation**: Major events (betrayal, loss) permanently rewrite personality rules, thresholds, and weights — characters grow and change with story events.

### 3. Enterprise-Grade Audit Engine
- **Plugin-Based Architecture**: Customizable checkpoints for different scenarios.
- **Consistency Audit**: Ensures responses match current personality, state, and role — prevents OOC (Out-of-Character) behavior.
- **Compliance & Safety**: Rules-based filtering, sensitive content blocking, and standardized output.
- **Full Traceability**: Every decision includes responsibility account, timestamp, and audit report.

### 4. Standardized Integration
- Outputs ready-to-use prompts compatible with **Kimi, ChatGPT, Dify, Coze, LangChain**, and any LLM platform.
- Fully serializable state for database storage, memory persistence, and cross-session continuity.
